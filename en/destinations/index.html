Destinations

Southern Chile, at your own pace

Carretera Austral

1,240 km of gravel and forest connecting Puerto Montt to Villa O'Higgins — the reason our campers are built 4x4, manual, and tough.

Torres del Paine

Chile's most iconic national park, reachable via Puerto Natales at the southern end of the Patagonia loop.

Marble Caves

Turquoise water carved through centuries-old marble on Lago General Carrera — best reached by boat from Puerto Río Tranquilo.

Glaciers

Ancient ice fields visible from both the Chilean and Argentine sides of the Andes.

Native Forests

Alerce and araucaria groves, some of the oldest trees on the continent, along the Carretera Austral's national parks.

Wildlife

Guanacos, condors, and — if you're lucky and patient — a wild puma at dawn or dusk.

Whale Watching

Humpback and southern right whales pass through Patagonian waters — boat trips available from several coastal towns.

Penguin Colonies

Magellanic penguin colonies dot the southern coastline, an easy detour on your way toward Torres del Paine.

Cross the Border

Chile today, Argentina tomorrow

Our camper trucks are set up for the paperwork required to cross into Argentina, so the full Patagonia loop is genuinely open to you — El Chaltén, El Calafate, and the Argentine side of Torres del Paine, not just the Chilean half.

Border-crossing rentals require the higher CLP $2,000,000 security deposit — ask us when you book and we'll handle the paperwork.
