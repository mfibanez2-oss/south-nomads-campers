The Camper Blog

Everything we know about traveling Chile and Patagonia in a 4x4 camper truck — written between trips, in Puerto Varas, by the people who build and drive these campers.

We rent 4x4 camper trucks from Puerto Varas, in the Chilean Lake District, and we drive the same roads we rent for. This blog is where we put everything travelers keep asking us on WhatsApp: how many days the Carretera Austral really takes, which ferries you must book months ahead, where you can legally sleep, what a camper rental in Chile actually costs, and when it is worth crossing into Argentina — and when it is not.

No sponsored fluff, no AI-generated route lists that ignore the ferry timetable. Just what has worked, what has broken, and what we would do differently.
