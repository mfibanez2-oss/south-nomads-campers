Itineraries

A sample route down the Carretera Austral

A starting point — every trip we plan gets adjusted to your dates, pace, and which camper you book.

10-Day Route · Puerto Varas → Coyhaique
01

Puerto Varas → Puerto Varas

Pickup, camper walkthrough, first night at a lakeside campsite near Puerto Varas.

02–03

Hornopirén → Caleta Gonzalo

Ferry crossings begin. Enter Pumalín Park — alerce forests, waterfalls, volcanic landscapes.

04–05

Chaitén → Puyuhuapi

Coastal fjord views and hot springs at Puyuhuapi. Queulat National Park's hanging glacier.

06–07

Puerto Cisnes → Puerto Río Tranquilo

Boat out to the Marble Caves on Lago General Carrera — one of the trip's highlights.

08–09

Cerro Castillo

Trekking day around the Cerro Castillo massif, one of the Carretera Austral's best hikes.

10

Coyhaique

Drop-off in Coyhaique, or continue south toward Puerto Natales and Torres del Paine with more days.
