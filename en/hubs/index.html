Rental Hubs

Pick up where your Patagonia trip begins

Puerto Varas is our home base — but we regularly coordinate pickup and drop-off further south for travelers doing a one-way Carretera Austral trip.

Main Hub Free

Puerto Varas

Our home base in the Los Lagos Region. Every rental starts here with a full camper walkthrough, no extra fee. Pickups 10 AM–5 PM, returns before 4 PM.

Puerto Montt Airport

CLP $35,000

Flying in? We can meet you at Puerto Montt's El Tepual Airport (PMC) for a fixed coordination fee.

Coyhaique (Balmaceda Airport)

CLP $600,000

Halfway down the Carretera Austral. A popular drop-off point for travelers continuing overland into Argentina, or starting their trip further south.

Puerto Natales

CLP $900,000

The gateway to Torres del Paine. Drop off here after a one-way Patagonia expedition and fly out, or continue toward Argentine Patagonia.

One-way fees apply for pickup or drop-off outside Puerto Varas — same rate whichever direction you're headed. Ask us to confirm at the time of booking.
